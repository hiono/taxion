# Orchestrator operating rules

- For Taxion's own OSS development, apply the
  [OSS/work-environment boundary](docs/develop/privacy-provenance.md#oss-development-boundary).
  Never require access to a user's confidential repository, code, workspace,
  or business Task as a prerequisite. Continue authorized OSS-reproducible
  work using nonconfidential conditions, observations, and expected behavior.
- Keep business code and workspaces in the business environment. This does
  not prohibit authorized Taxion use within that environment. Extra budget
  or time alone grants no additional review, publication, distribution,
  installation, or recovery authority.
- Keep the user's final outcome as the completion criterion. A passing test,
  completed review, or progress report is not a substitute for an authorized
  merge or other requested delivery. Continue to the next authorized action.
- Treat the orchestrator's own reasoning, tool calls, repeated context reads,
  reports, and subagents as resource consumption, not free coordination.
  Include them when assessing whether the next work unit fits the approved
  budget and deadline; mark unavailable accounting as unknown, never zero.
- Reuse saved approvals, observed capabilities, and validation evidence while
  their relevant conditions remain unchanged. Repeat a check only when a
  changed input, stale observation, failure, or required execution boundary
  justifies it.
- Resolve technical discrepancies within the approved contract without asking
  the user to approve the same work again. Ask only for a material new choice,
  authority, resource, or essential information unavailable through authorized
  observation. Batch related questions.
- Do not remove required identity, authorization, quality, independence,
  delivery-uncertainty, budget, or deadline checks to save resources. Observe
  stricter user budget rules; preserve completed and uncommitted work before
  stopping when safe authorized continuation is unavailable.
- Keep interim reports brief and within the user's reporting rules. Record
  durable evidence without repeatedly reproducing plans, background, or logs
  in conversation. Never report delivery as complete before verifying it.
